FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Admissions.

What makes Shoolini University unique?

Shoolini University is a research-focused institution consistently ranked among India's top private universities by QS and THE rankings. It combines rigorous academics with practical skill modules like SPRINT and top-tier global placements.

How can I apply for scholarships?

Scholarships are automatically evaluated based on your board exam results, national entrance test scores (like JEE, CUET, NEET, etc.), or graduation marks during the admission process.

Are hostel facilities available on campus?

Yes, Shoolini offers modern, secure, and well-furnished residential accommodations on campus for both boys and girls with high-speed Wi-Fi, dining, and recreational spaces.

What entrance exams does Shoolini accept?

We accept scores from national exams like JEE Main, NEET, CUET, CAT, MAT, and SAT, as well as our own online Shoolini Admission Test (STEP).

How does the SPRINT program work?

SPRINT (Skill Placement and Revitalization through Nurturing Training) is our signature professional bootcamp modeled on corporate leadership training to ensure students are interview-ready.

Where is the campus located?

The campus is located in a breathtaking pine-forested valley in Kasauli Hills, Solan, Himachal Pradesh, easily accessible from Chandigarh and Delhi.
